Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or API key?

No registration or user-supplied API key is required. Data relies on third-party providers and your network connection.

Can I place trades?

Candle displays and analyzes market data. It is not an order execution platform and does not provide investment advice.

Is every market real time?

No. Providers have different delays and update schedules and may throttle or interrupt service. Displayed prices are not guaranteed real-time exchange quotes.
